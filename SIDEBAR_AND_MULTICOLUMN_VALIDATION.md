# Sidebar and Multi Column Validation

The collapsed sidebar presents the team identity, sign-out, and dark-mode controls as three vertically aligned 36 × 36 pixel icon buttons. Their text labels remain available in the expanded sidebar and each compact control preserves its accessible name.

The Multi column section now exposes only Feature, Service, and Product collection content styles. When enabled, its **See All** control renders after the complete column grid as a full-width neutral secondary action, distinct from the dark primary action and the outlined tertiary treatment.
