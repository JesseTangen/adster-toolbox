# Sidebar and Multi Column Validation

The collapsed sidebar presents the team identity, sign-out, and dark-mode controls as three vertically aligned 36 × 36 pixel icon buttons. Their text labels remain available in the expanded sidebar and each compact control preserves its accessible name.

The Multi column section exposes Feature, Blog, Service, Product collection, and Product content styles. When enabled, its **See All** control renders after the complete column grid as a full-width neutral secondary action, distinct from the dark primary action and the outlined tertiary treatment. Blog preserves image, author, summary, and optional Read more treatment; Product preserves image, price, and an outlined tertiary Add to cart treatment.
